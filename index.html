Our Story

The Story of Rytham Veg

At Rytham Veg, we believe that food is more than just a meal, it is a melody of tradition, health, and soul. Rooted in the rich culinary heritage of South India, we bring you a dining experience that honors the past while catering to the modern, health-conscious palate. Whether you are a lifelong fan of Indian cuisine or a curious first-timer, our doors are open to everyone looking for pure veg and healthy options that never compromise on taste.

Catering Services

Rytham welcomes you to experience the finest 100% vegetarian cuisine for your next event, gathering, or party orders. We specialize in birthday parties, housewarming ceremonies, baby showers, and all kinds of events. We also customize menus according to your needs. Delicious Menu

  • Rytham Special Thali

    Special

    Grand South Indian feast with variety rice, curries, kootu, poriyal, sambar, rasam, appalam & dessert.

  • Veg Briyani

    Popular

    Aromatic basmati rice cooked with fresh seasonal vegetables, mint, and rich South Indian spices.

  • Rava Dosa

    Crispy

    Lacy, golden semolina crepe tempered with cumin, ginger & green chilies, served with chutneys & sambar.

  • Poori with Masala

    Fluffy, golden puffed wheat breads served with warmly spiced potato masala and coconut chutney.

  • Ulunthu Rice with Karakuzhmbu

    Traditional

    Nutritious black urad dal rice served with authentic spicy & tangy village-style tamarind curry.

  • Honey Garlic Cauliflower

    New

    Crispy wok-tossed cauliflower florets glazed in a savory, aromatic sweet honey-garlic sauce.

  • Veg Kola Urundai

    Starter

    Crunchy, golden fried spiced vegetable & lentil dumplings seasoned with fennel and crushed coconut.

  • Chilli Parata

    Spicy

    Flaky shredded parotta tossed on hot griddle with crunchy capsicum, onions, and bold chilli masala.

  • Karuppu Kavuni Rice Halwa

    Dessert

    Traditional black rice halwa made with Karuppu Kavuni rice, rich in natural iron & fibre, slow-cooked with pure ghee and jaggery.

  • Karupaati Coffee

    Beverage

    Freshly brewed South Indian filter decoction sweetened naturally with palm jaggery and a hint of dry ginger.
